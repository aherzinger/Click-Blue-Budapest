# Valyo: existing projects and stakeholders already involved

Source of this note: https://valyo.hu/fauszodak-budapesten-flaszter/ and the linked
project pages on valyo.hu, read on 10 November 2026.

Valyo (Valyo – Város és Folyó Egyesület, founded 2010) is the Budapest NGO whose work
overlaps most with Click-Blue-Budapest. Treat it as a partner, not a competitor.

## The page that started this note: "Fauszodák Budapesten" (2023)

- Public-art installation and programme in the Flaszter I. Budapesti Köztéri Kortárs
  Művészeti Biennálé, 22 Sep to 23 Oct 2023, about 2,000 participants, 3 organisers,
  budget HUF 1.99 m.
- An upside-down "floating bath" installation next to the Vigadó boat station on Jane
  Haining rakpart, plus SKUBI peep-boxes marking former floating-bath sites along both
  embankments (Fővám tér, Raoul Wallenberg rkp., Friedrich Born rkp., Várkert rkp.,
  Sztehlo Gábor rkp., Bem rkp., Margit híd Buda end, id. Antall József rkp.).
- Stated goal: build the first Budapest floating bath of the 21st century in 2024 and get
  Budapest onto the "swimmable cities" list. The 2024 target was not met.
- Historic framing used by Valyo: floating baths ("fauszoda") existed from the 1810s to
  WWII, then were banned; public Danube bathing returned with the Római szabadstrand.

## What is missing from Valyo's network, and where we can add value

- No university as a formal partner (BME, ELTE appear only as a lab service).
- No passenger-boat association (MAHOSZ, Személyhajósok Szövetsége); only MAHART.
- No kayak or canoe federation, only individual clubs.
- No tech, data or hackathon partner.
- No EIT or EU innovation funding since DANUrB.
- No thermal-water element in any project; BGYH is a client, not a thermal partner.
- The Fauszoda page is explicitly looking for partners.

These gaps are the natural entry points for Click-Blue-Budapest.
